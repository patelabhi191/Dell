# Repository guide (Frontier in 60)

- `video-studio/` is the channel's video pipeline (faceless animated shorts for Instagram and
  YouTube). **Before any video work, read `video-studio/PLAYBOOK.md`**: it is the standing brief
  for style, voice, script structure, workflow and the pre-publish checklist. Technical
  reference is `video-studio/README.md`.
- New videos follow the playbook's brief (section 8) and one of its two templates
  (informative or story). Check every factual claim before rendering.
- Reference clips live in `video-studio/references/` and are for study only; never reuse their
  footage, script lines or characters.
- `Space Shooter/` is an unrelated Unity project. Leave it alone unless asked.
