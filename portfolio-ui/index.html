Market backdrop (mid-November 2026)

  • AI stocks are driving the swings. The S&P 500 fell 1.9% and the Nasdaq 2.5% on Nov 16, though the S&P was still above its Nov 5 close. Concentration is high: the ten largest companies are about 38% of the index.
  • Big catalysts ahead: Nvidia earnings and the return of official US economic data. Rising Treasury yields are the main risk to valuations.
  • Large caps trade near 21.5x forward earnings. Small caps (S&P 600) are near 16x, which is why the plan holds some small-cap exposure.
  • Wall Street year-end S&P 500 targets of about 8,000 were set earlier in the year. The index was reported near 7,700 in early November.

These notes come from a quick web search. Several results were undated or from 2025, so check levels on a live quote source before trading. Sources: AP via ADN, Reuters via Khaleej Times, Beinsure, Morningstar.
