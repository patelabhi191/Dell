Past sells — aligning the ink

The table's box is already flush. Commit 6113468 pinned it to within 1px of the summary tiles and the footnote, and the tests check exactly that. What is still off is the ink: the first column's text sits 9px inside the panel and the ✕ stops 17px short of the right edge, while the heading, the From / To labels and the footnote all start on 0.

The rule being applied: a child of this panel declares the panel's edge either with a border or with its ink. The tiles and the date inputs may sit inset — each carries a 1px stroke that lands on 0, so the edge is still drawn where it belongs and the inset reads as the inside of a control. The table is the only child with no line at 0, so for it the text is the edge. That is why the tiles stay at 14px and the inputs at 10px in the proposed column: chasing 0 for those would be the actual mistake.

Each frame below is the real 384px right-hand Dashboard column. Nothing in the app has been changed to make this page — the proposed rules are injected at render time.
