Each deposit is filed under the year you pick, and repeat deposits in the same year add up. The log is editable — remove any entry with ✕, then re-add it with the correct amount or year. Annual limits default to the real CRA amount for whichever year you're viewing (TFSA varies by year; FHSA is C$8,000 since 2023). A new year is added automatically on Jan 1; until CRA announces that year's indexed TFSA limit, the previous year's figure is carried forward as a placeholder \u2014 update it when the official number lands — raise the limit field if you have carry-forward room (FHSA allows up to C$8,000 carried forward, so up to C$16,000 in a year; TFSA room accumulates since 2009). FHSA lifetime maximum is C$40,000. Withdrawals do not restore contribution room in the current year, so the log intentionally only tracks deposits. Check CRA My Account for your exact numbers.
