## Fact check (re-checked 2026-11-18)

Verified:
- Microsoft 365 (Nov 12): outage began Nov 12 (~16:17 UTC / 11:17 a.m. ET), ran into Nov 13-14; Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive hit; traced to an earlier network capacity upgrade; no malicious activity. CNBC, CIO, ABC News.
- FAA (Nov 13, a Friday): router installs at an FAA radio site in El Dorado, AR (copper-to-fiber work) overloaded the network; Amarillo, TX radar site also named; 17 of 22 centers affected; FAA paused Juniper router installs; no sign of cyberattack. Engadget, ABC News, Star-Advertiser (AP), The Flight Brief.
- Chase (Nov 16): "temporary issue tied to a software bug", not cyber-related; Wells Fargo / Bank of America customers had trouble with Zelle transfers to Chase. Al Jazeera, ABC7, WBTV.
- United (Nov 16): pilots could not get final aircraft weights; departures delayed; no indication of cyber. Fox 4, Gulf News, Aviation Business ME.
- NSA (Nov 17): "temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance"; not a cyberattack. CBS News, ABC News (AP), Al Jazeera.
- Apple (Nov 17): internal software update caused iCloud, Apple Pay, App Store outages; not a cyberattack. UPI, TIME, 9to5Mac.
- PlayStation Network (Nov 18): sign-in/connection outage hours before GTA VI's launch; Sony has not given a cause. Android Authority, GV Wire.
- DNI Jay Clayton: outages separate, "associated with maintenance and technical changes"; with DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin and FBI Deputy Director Christopher Raia told Congress on Nov 18 there is no evidence of a coordinated cyberattack. PBS NewsHour, Yahoo News, ABC7 Chicago.
