## Caption

One small box stopped every plane in America from taking off. 📦✈️

On Nov 13, 2026, the FAA halted every departure for more than four hours. Only the third nationwide ground stop ever. The first was 9/11.

It wasn't hackers. During routine work to switch copper lines to fiber, one router flooded the air traffic network with over 1,000 connection attempts every five seconds. 17 of the 22 en-route control centers started losing their lines.

The scary part? That network was ordered in 2002, before the iPhone existed. And in 2009, a router mistake on the same network knocked out 129 air traffic facilities.

Congress has approved $12.5 billion to fix air traffic control. Would you trust the old system until then? Comment yes or no 👇
