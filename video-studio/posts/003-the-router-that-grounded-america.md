## Caption

One small box stopped every plane in America from taking off. 📦✈️

On Nov 13, 2026, the FAA halted every departure in the US for more than four hours. It was only the third nationwide ground stop ever. The first was 9/11.

The cause wasn't a cyberattack. During routine work to switch copper lines to fiber at one FAA site, a single router started flooding the air traffic network with over 1,000 connection attempts every five seconds. Controllers at 17 of the 22 en-route centers began losing their communication lines. No talking, no takeoffs.

Investigators are still working out whether the router was faulty or installed wrong.
