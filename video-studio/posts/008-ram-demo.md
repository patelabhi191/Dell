## Fact check of the reference (2026-11-22)

| Claim in the reference | Verdict | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RAM costs 4x | True at chip level | DDR5 16Gb chip spot price ~$6.84 (Sep 2025) to ~$27.20 (Dec 2025), Tom's Hardware price index. Retail 32GB kits rose more (~5x by late 2026), figures vary by source. |
| One AI company "promised to buy up to 40% of the world's RAM" | Overstated | OpenAI signed letters of intent (not contracts) with Samsung and SK Hynix on Oct 1, 2025 for up to 900,000 DRAM wafers/month. The 40% is a TechInsights estimate against 2025 output; timeline not set (one official cited 2029). |
| The GPU maker funds it so it buys its GPUs | Outdated | Nvidia's Sep 22, 2025 letter of intent (up to $100B) was never made binding; replaced on Feb 27, 2026 by a $30B Nvidia equity stake in OpenAI's $110B round. |
| AI needs $2T/yr by 2030, $800B short | True | Bain & Company Global Technology Report, Sep 23, 2025. |
| "Non-existent money", "demand doesn't exist", "mathematically impossible profit" | Opinion | Rhetoric; Bain describes a shortfall, not an impossibility. |

Demo lines use the checked versions: "RAM chip prices nearly quadrupled in three months";
"OpenAI's deal could take up to forty percent of the world's memory" (shown as an analyst
estimate); "the GPU maker is investing in its own customer" ($30B stake).
